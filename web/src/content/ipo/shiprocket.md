As the Shiprocket IPO enters its open stage, set to run from 12 to 14 August with an estimated listing thereafter, market indicators provide a preliminary gauge for investor interest. The grey-market premium (GMP) of approximately 34.0% of the upper band price (translating to around ₹33 on a ₹97 upper band) suggests the market anticipates a listing gain. However, it is crucial to approach this indicator with caution, as GMP is unofficial, unregulated, thinly traded, and highly volatile, capable of fluctuating significantly before listing.

The subscription demand, now visible, paints a detailed picture of investor appetite. With an overall subscription of 102.28 times, the issue has garnered substantial interest. Breaking down the demand, Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIBs) have subscribed 125.2 times, indicating strong institutional conviction. Non-Institutional Investors/High Net Worth Individuals (NII/HNI) have subscribed 92.58 times, suggesting significant participation from this segment, potentially leveraging their investment. Retail investors, with a 48.38 times subscription, show notable but relatively more subdued interest compared to the other segments. This split implies that while there is broad interest, institutional and leveraged HNI investment are driving the bulk of the demand.

The issue size of ₹1617.48 Cr positions Shiprocket for a Mainboard listing, distinguishing it from smaller, less liquid SME listings. Mainboard listings typically offer greater liquidity and lower risk due to their larger size, which could attract a broader investor base and provide more stable trading post-listing.
