As the Sunshine Pictures IPO enters its open stage, scheduled to run from 18 to 20 August with an estimated listing on 28 August, market indicators provide some insight into investor expectations. The grey-market premium (GMP) of approximately 20.3% of the upper band price (₹73 over ₹360) suggests that the market anticipates a listing gain. However, it is crucial to approach this indicator with caution, as GMP is unofficial, unregulated, thinly traded, and highly volatile, capable of evaporating before listing.

With the issue currently open for bidding, the subscription demand is now visible. The overall issue was subscribed 2.45 times, with the Qualified Institutional Buyers (QIB) portion subscribed 1.32 times, Non-Institutional Investors (NIIs)/High Net Worth Individuals (HNIs) at 4.21 times, and the retail segment at 1.89 times. This split indicates that while there is some institutional interest, the demand is more pronounced among leveraged HNIs, potentially driven by the pursuit of short-term gains rather than purely institutional conviction or broad retail enthusiasm.

The issue size for Sunshine Pictures, listing on the Mainboard, contextualizes it as a larger and more liquid offering compared to SME listings. This structural aspect generally aligns with lower risk and higher liquidity post-listing, a consideration for investors prioritizing market accessibility.
