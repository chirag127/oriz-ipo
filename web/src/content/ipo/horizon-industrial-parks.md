Horizon Industrial Parks is pursuing a Mainboard listing with an issue size of ₹2600.04 Cr. This distinction is noteworthy because Mainboard listings are generally characterized by larger issue sizes and greater liquidity compared to SME (Small and Medium Enterprises) board listings, which are typically smaller, thinner in trading, and associated with higher risk due to lower liquidity and higher lot values. The Mainboard listing status of Horizon Industrial Parks suggests a more substantial and potentially more stable market presence post-listing.
